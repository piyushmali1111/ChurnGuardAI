# ChurnGuardAI E-Commerce Customer Support Knowledge Base

## Shipping & Delivery Policy
- Standard domestic shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days.
- International shipping to Canada and Europe via FedEx Express takes 2 to 4 business days.
- If a package tracking status shows 'pending' for over 5 days, customer support automatically escalates a carrier investigation ticket and offers complimentary priority reshipment.

## Returns, Exchange & Damaged Items Policy
- We offer free returns and size exchanges within 30 days of purchase date.
- If an item arrives crushed, broken, or damaged in transit, we issue immediate store credit or a full refund, plus a courtesy discount code for their next order with reinforced priority packaging.

## Subscriptions & Replenishments Policy
- Customers can pause, skip, or cancel recurring monthly subscription replenishments anytime under Account > Subscriptions.
- If a customer inquires about pausing due to cost, support representatives can offer a 15% discount on their next 3 monthly refill cycles.

## Discount Codes & Checkout Promotions
- Discount codes are limited to one per order and cannot be combined with clearance pricing.
- If a customer experiences a checkout discount code failure during a sale, support automatically credits an equivalent 15% discount directly to their account.
